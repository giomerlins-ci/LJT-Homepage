I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), where I am advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/) at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/).

Prior to joining HKUST, I obtained my B.Eng. degree from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

Research Interests
------
My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with particular interests in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** — Synthesizing verifiable reasoning data and studying inference-time reinforcement learning methods
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — Investigating the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — Discovering universal truthfulness hyperplanes inside LLMs and using inner representations for hallucination mitigation
